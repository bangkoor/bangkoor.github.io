About Me

I am a quantitative spatial analyst with expertise in remote sensing, spatial statistics, and algorithm development. My research focuses on developing and validating computational methods for the analysis of multi-scalar spatial data, aimed at addressing complex environmental challenges with empirical rigor.

Combining core principles of geography with modern spatial intelligence, my work emphasizes reproducibility, robust statistical modeling, and evidence-based synthesis in geographic information science.

Remote Sensing

Multispectral image processing, algorithm development, and validation

Geocomputation

Advanced spatial topology and programmatic spatial workflows

Spatial Statistics

Geostatistics, spatial econometrics, and spatial-temporal modeling

Environmental Modeling

Systematic resilience modeling and empirical impact assessment

Research Areas

Environmental Parameterization

Applying multi-temporal earth observation algorithms to quantify biophysical dynamics, biomass flux, and ecological integrity.

Urban Spatial Morphology

Employing robust spatial analytics to model urban sprawl trajectories, land-cover transition probabilities, and geometric sprawl indices.

Spatial Climatology

Synthesizing spatial downscaling of climate datasets to analyze localized meteorological anomalies and map multidimensional vulnerabilities.

Hydro-Spatial Modeling

Integrating hydrological equations with DEM-derived spatial parameters to systematically model watershed delineation and flow dynamics.
